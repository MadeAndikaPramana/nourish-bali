Notes:
- None of the interiors are NOURISH. Unsplash lists them in George Town, Penang (`space-airy`), Seoul (`space-plants`,
  `space-bar`), Brooklyn (`space-rattan`) and Florence (`pantry`). They were picked to match the white-wall, timber,
  rattan and plant look of the cafés' own Instagram, not to pass as the real rooms.
- `canggu-ricefield.jpg` was taken in Canggu, not at the Berawa café. `uluwatu-cliffs.jpg` shows the Uluwatu
  coastline, not the cafés.
- `wrap.jpg` is a generic wrap and `pizza.jpg` a generic margherita-style pizza, not NOURISH dishes.
- Rejected: photos that Unsplash tags as other real venues (cafés in Tibubeneng and Canggu, a Bali surf camp, and
  cafés in Miami, Washington DC, Dordrecht and Surabaya), plus one with a visible sportswear logo.

## Fonts

Self-hosted via Fontsource, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1:
- **Archivo** (Omnibus-Type), wordmark and signage-style labels
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Tokotype, Jakarta), body
- **Instrument Serif** (Instrument), editorial headings

## Code and effects

- Motion: [Motion](https://motion.dev) (MIT), smooth scroll: [Lenis](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis) (MIT).
- Some interaction patterns are adapted from free [Skiper UI](https://skiper-ui.com) components (scroll-drawn SVG path,
  text roll navigation, progressive blur) and rewritten for this site.

Link-preview images in `public/og/` are screenshots of this site's own pages (`npm run og`), so they contain the same
placeholder photos.
